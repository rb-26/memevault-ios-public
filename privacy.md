# MemeVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

MemeVault does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data. The developer receives nothing from your use of the app.

## What the app stores

- The images you add, plus text recognized in them, descriptions, tags, notes, collections and favorites.
- This data lives on your device, in a container shared only between MemeVault and its own keyboard, share and iMessage extensions.

## iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, your library syncs through your private iCloud (CloudKit) database. Only you can access it. It is governed by Apple's terms and your iCloud settings, and the developer cannot see it.

## On-device processing

Text recognition (Apple Vision), image classification and AI descriptions (Apple Foundation Models) run entirely on your device. Images are not sent to any server.

## Keyboard and Full Access

The MemeVault keyboard asks for "Allow Full Access" only so it can place the selected image on the clipboard. It does not log keystrokes, make network requests, or transmit anything.

## Share extension

When you share an image or link to MemeVault, the app saves the image. If you share a web link to an image, the app downloads that image directly from the address you shared.

## Third parties

MemeVault contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no third-party services.

## Backups

Export creates a zip file that you choose where to save. The developer never receives it.

## Children

MemeVault does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
