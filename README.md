# MemeVault

A private library for your memes and reaction images, for iPhone.

Save a meme once, find it in seconds, send it from any app.

## Features

- **Save from anywhere:** Photos, Files, clipboard, or the share sheet in Safari and other apps. GIFs stay animated.
- **On-device text recognition:** every image is read on your phone, so you can search by the words in it.
- **AI descriptions and tags:** generated on device with Apple Intelligence where available.
- **Semantic search:** finds memes that mean the same as your words, not just exact matches.
- **Keyboard and iMessage:** tap a meme in the MemeVault keyboard and paste, or send straight from the iMessage app drawer.
- **Organize:** favorites, collections, editable tags, bulk actions, duplicate finder, Recently Deleted (30 days).
- **Caption editor:** add top and bottom text and save it as a new meme.
- **iCloud sync:** your library follows you across your devices through your own iCloud account.
- **Backup:** export everything to one zip and import it again.

## Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Everything stays on your device and in your own iCloud. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).

## Requirements

iPhone with iOS 26 or later. AI descriptions need a device that supports Apple Intelligence; search and everything else work without it.
